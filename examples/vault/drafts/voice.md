A draft is allowed to be metronomic. Rhythm is a revision problem, so the
uniformity check is off in here and turns back on the moment a piece leaves this
directory.

Triplets get a longer leash too: one per three hundred words rather than one per
two hundred, because a first pass reaches for a list and the fix is cheap later.
