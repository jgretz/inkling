# On Writing With an Agent

The trouble with most writing tools that bolt on a model is that they answer a
question nobody asked. They offer to finish your sentence. What a writer
actually wants, most of the time, is an argument.

## What the panels are for

The left panel is the piece as a reader will meet it. The middle is the piece as
you are making it. The right is someone to make it with.

- The **preview** tracks the editor keystroke by keystroke, so there is never a
  moment where what you see is behind what you wrote.
- The **editor** is raw markdown, on purpose. Syntax you can see is syntax you
  can trust.
- The **agent** sees exactly what the context strip says it sees. Nothing else.

> A tool that hides what it sends is a tool you have to argue with twice.

## What is still missing

Everything interesting, really. The agent cannot yet read the document, propose
an edit, or hold an opinion across sessions.
